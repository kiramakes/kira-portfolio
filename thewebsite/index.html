About

Code with curiosity

I've been building for the web for over 7 years — from frontend interfaces to full-stack systems handling real traffic at scale.

These days I focus on the intersection of interactive design and engineering: WebGL experiences, scroll-driven narratives, and interfaces that reward exploration and feel alive.

I believe the best software doesn't just work — it reacts to you, adapts to your context, and leaves an impression. That's what I chase.

When not shipping code, I'm studying emerging web technologies, contributing to open source, or hunting for the next challenge.
